The Bubbly Gem

Junior Marketing Associate (Intern)

The Bubbly Gem is a fast-fashion label with a new product launch every week. Working directly with the founder in a small team, I planned, designed and ran launch campaigns, made the daily content for Instagram and TikTok, shot and edited product images, and kept the online store up to date.

Each week I reported on performance using Meta Business Suite, Shopify and TikTok analytics, and those reports shaped what we did next.

Campaign

Go Green

I proposed Go Green to strengthen the brand's image and meet growing demand for eco-friendly products. The campaign introduced The Bubbly Gem's new recyclable bag and showed customers simple ways to cut plastic and paper waste, such as returning garment bags for reuse and choosing an e-receipt.

12%increase in social media engagement in the first month

7%growth in sales of the new recyclable bag in the first month

Social media

Instagram feed

I kept the feed visually cohesive by working within the brand's colour palette and aesthetic. Engagement rates and customer feedback told me what the audience responded to, and I made more of it.

Social media

Instagram and Facebook Stories

I made Stories more engaging with polls, questions and links. These gave followers a reason to respond, which raised the brand's visibility and sent more traffic to the store.

E-commerce and reporting

Shopify store

I created the product listings (names, details, images, variations and pricing), organised products into collections such as New Arrivals so they were easier to find, and set up discount codes.

I tracked checkout conversion, cart abandonment and time on site to find where the store's design and layout could improve. I also reported top-performing products and colours to the research and development team to guide future launches.

Meta Business Suite

I scheduled each month's content in advance so posts went out in a planned, consistent order. Its analytics on reach, engagement and audience behaviour showed what customers responded to and shaped the following month's content.
